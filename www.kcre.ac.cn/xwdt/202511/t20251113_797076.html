加德满都中心举办首期中尼环境研究联合研讨会

2021-09-16

    9月5日，中国科学院加德满都科教中心（以下简称“加德满都中心”）以线下线上相结合的方式举办了围绕大断面计划的首期中尼环境研究联合研讨会。会议由加德满都中心执行主任汪涛研究员和特里布文大学水文气象系主任Deepak Aryal教授（尼泊尔科学院院士）共同主持，设加德满都主会场及北京、成都两个分会场，来自青藏高原所、成都山地所、特里布文大学、国际山地综合发展中心等单位的50余名专家学者参加会议。

 
会议围绕气候变化、环境污染、冰湖溃决等研究领域，邀请马耀明研究员、熊东红研究员、张强弓研究员，特里布文大学Deepak Aryal教授、Kedar Rijal教授、Ramesh Pant博士，国际山地综合发展中心Arun Shrestha博士、Nakul Chettri博士以及成都山地所Nitesh Khadka分别作报告。
 
此次研讨会增进了中尼科学研究与人才交流合作，推动了加德满都中心后续“大断面”计划开展及冰湖溃决研究。汪涛研究员表示，希望疫情早日结束，以便更多国内科学家能够前往尼泊尔开展现场考察及研究工作，同时，要通过举办各类研讨会的方式，进一步增进双方青年科学家的交流。
 
 
 
KCRE Holds Sino-Nepal Joint Seminar on Environmental Research 
 
On September 5th, the Sino-Nepal Joint Seminar on Environmental Research was held by the Kathmandu Center for Research and Education, CAS-TU (hereinafter the "KCRE"). The seminar was co-chaired by Professor WANG Tao, Executive Director of KCRE, and Professor Deepak Aryal (member of the Nepal Academy of Science and Technology), Head of the Central Department of Hydrometeorology and Meteorology (CDHM) of Tribhuvan University. More than 50 participants from the Institute of Tibetan Plateau Research (ITP), the Institute of Mountain Hazards and Environment (IMHE), Tribhuvan University (TU), the International Center for Integrated Mountain Development (ICIMOD) and other institutions attended.
 
Professors MA Yaoming, XIONG Donghong, and ZHANG Qianggong of CAS, Professors Deepak Aryal, Kedar Rijal, and Dr. Ramesh Pant of Tribhuvan University, Dr. Arun Shrestha and Dr. Nakul Chettri of ICIMOD, and Nitesh Khadka of IMHE presented their research focusing on climate change, environmental pollution, and glacier lake outburst.
 
During this seminar, further Sino-Nepal cooperation in scientific research and talent exchanges was promoted and future collaboration in cross-section and glacier lake outburst research was proposed. Professor WANG Tao commented that he hopes the pandemic could end soon so that Chinese scientists would be able to travel to Nepal to carry out fieldwork and research. At the same time, it is necessary to further enhance exchange between young scientists from both sides through seminars and conferences.
